Chapter 7 Season 4: Override

Fortnite is a free-to-play battle royale from Epic Games where 100 players drop onto an island and fight to be the last one standing, combining shooting, building, and constant crossovers with characters from across pop culture. The current season: Chapter 7 Season 4: Override launched August 20, 2026 with a video games theme built around “breaking the rules.”

Season 4: Override

Players activate Override Consoles scattered around the map to rewrite the rules for the whole server, enter Lobby Hacks (cheat codes) for rewards, and battle across the island as it morphs into gaming worlds like Sonic’s Green Hill Zone. It headlines with over 20 gaming crossovers Sonic the Hedgehog, Mega Man, Kingdom Hearts, Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, and more with Sonic fronting the Battle Pass. The season runs through October 31, 2026.

What's New?

Override Consoles

Consoles spawn in every match, and the first player to reach one can rewrite the rules for the whole server server-wide buffs like Sonic Speed, extra XP, Overshield, or an Extra Life. Activating one broadcasts your location and puts your name on the console base, so everyone knows who’s in control.

Feature 2

A hidden Admin Panel in the lobby lets you enter classic-style cheat codes for rewards like 'OverrideXP' for 40,000 XP or 'GottaGoFast' for a guaranteed Sonic Sprite. More codes get discovered throughout the season, plus hidden cheat-code arrows around the map that grant random Sprites.

A Map That Morphs Into Game Worlds

Geno’s game cartridge is overriding the island, warping parts of the map into video game dimensions race through Sonic’s Green Hill Zone with loop-de-loops and golden rings, dodge Tetris blocks, and grab new gear like the Mega Buster arm cannon and the 8-Bit Shotgun.
